About FruitOS

FruitOS runs the complete wiring diagram of a fruit fly's central nervous system as a live simulation. The fly's behaviour on screen is decided only by its own command neurons.

Brain data: . Published 2026 in Cell ("Sexual dimorphism in the complete Drosophila male central nervous system connectome"); data at male-cns.janelia.org.

Model: leaky integrate-and-fire network after Shiu et al. 2024 (Nature), recalibrated for this dataset, with spike-frequency adaptation. Ask the fly "how does this simulation work?" for its limits.

Software: FruitOS is free software (MIT). Built on Arch Linux; not affiliated with or endorsed by Arch Linux. 3D rendering by three.js (MIT).
